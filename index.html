Direct Post Compliance

The Creator-in-the-Loop Publishing Flow

In full compliance with TikTok's 2026 Content Sharing Guidelines, Zaitoon Studio eliminates unattended or headless public uploads in favor of complete creator transparency.

1

Artifact Discovery & Local Queue

Newly rendered video artifacts queue within the creator's local desktop studio. Zero media is transmitted across the internet without an interactive creator action.

2

Live OAuth Identity & Capability Query

The studio connects securely via TikTok Login Kit and executes /v2/post/publish/creator_info/query/. The creator's nickname, verified handle, profile avatar, and account-specific capabilities are rendered dynamically.

3

Full-Fidelity Video Preview

The creator plays the full vertical video locally in an HTML5 video player, ensuring narrative pacing, audio balance, and subtitle accuracy before initiating transfer.

4

Metadata & UTF-16 Rune Counter

The creator reviews and customizes captions, hashtags, and mentions. The interface enforces TikTok's 2,200 character boundary using strict UTF-16 code unit measurement (surrogate pairs and emojis accurately counted).

5

Dynamic Privacy Selection (No Silent Default)

In strict accordance with TikTok policy, privacy options are populated dynamically from the creator's live account permissions. No default privacy option is pre-selected; the creator must manually choose from the dropdown.

6

Interaction Controls & Greyed-Out Restrictions

Checkboxes for Duet, Stitch, and Comments start unchecked by default. If the creator's TikTok privacy settings prohibit an interaction, the toggle is automatically disabled and greyed out with an explanatory tooltip.

7

Commercial & Truthful AIGC Disclosures

Commercial Content Disclosure is turned OFF by default. When toggled, creators choose between "Your Brand" (Brand Organic) and "Branded Content" (Paid Partnership), automatically enforcing public visibility. AIGC labeling is truthfully set based on channel nature and creator confirmation.

8

Explicit Music Usage Consent & Transfer

The "Publish to TikTok" button remains disabled until the creator explicitly accepts TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation. Upon click, video chunks are transferred via FILE_UPLOAD and real-time status is polled via /publish/status/fetch/.

✅

No Headless / Unattended Public Uploads

Zaitoon Studio enforces an architectural Account Guard and SHA-256 idempotency store. Videos cannot be published headlessly from background jobs, preventing accidental duplicates and safeguarding creator brand integrity.
